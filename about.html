About Pineview

Local. Full-service. Straightforward.

Pineview is a local exterior contractor based in Post Falls, serving North Idaho and Eastern Washington.

Who We Are

One company for the whole outside of your home.

Most homeowners juggle a roofer, a gutter company, a siding crew, and a painter. That means four schedules, four quotes, and four phone numbers.

We do it differently. Pineview handles the whole exterior: roofing, gutters, siding, and exterior painting. We also install permanent and seasonal holiday lighting.

You get one point of contact. Your project is coordinated from start to finish. And we stand behind our work.
