FAQ

Common questions.

What areas do you serve?

Northern Idaho and Eastern Washington, including Post Falls, Coeur d'Alene, Spokane, and surrounding towns. Not sure if you're in range? Call us at 208-626-2145.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Pineview is licensed and insured in both Idaho and Washington. WA Reg. BRYCEDR740QC. ID Reg. 2981136.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the home, and give you a clear, flat quote. No cost and no obligation.

Can you help with storm damage and insurance claims?

Yes. We handle roof repair and replacement for storm damage and can work with your insurance claim. Call us and we'll start with an inspection.

Can you do my roof, gutters, siding, and paint together?

Yes. That's what we do best. A full exterior package means one crew, one schedule, and one point of contact for the whole job.

What's the difference between permanent and seasonal lighting?

Permanent lights are Govee lights mounted along your roofline that stay up year-round. You control colors, scenes, and schedules from an app, and they carry a 3-year Govee warranty. Seasonal lights are classic C9 Christmas lights we install for the holidays, service during the season, then take down and store.

Do I need Wi-Fi for permanent lights?

Yes, the lights connect to your home Wi-Fi. We handle the setup: we connect them, set up the Govee Home app, program your scenes and schedule, and show you how it all works.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes. We stand behind our workmanship with a warranty on every job. We'll walk you through the specific terms with your quote.
